        How points work

        Earn up to 1,000 points when every goal is complete:

        • Completion: 500 points.
        • Attempts: 200 points on your first run, minus 20 for each extra run, down to 0.
        • Safety: 100 points, minus 20 for each object or boundary hit, down to 0.
        • Independent work: 100 points, or 80 if you use a hint. Reopening a hint costs nothing extra.
        • Efficiency: up to 60 points for a short safe route and 40 for fewer executed rover commands. The reference route allows for obstacles. Comments and blank lines have no cost; movement speed does not affect points.

        Every press of Run after the briefing counts, including code errors and stopped runs. Resetting, switching missions, and refreshing keep your attempt, impact, and hint history. Your best completed score contributes to the expedition total. Existing completions earn points when you replay them.
